BHUNITI-LAB: ADVANCED AI-POWERED LAND GOVERNANCE, CAUSAL ECONOMETRICS, AND GIS SPATIAL DIGITAL TWIN PLATFORM
PROJECT SUMMARY AND EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW

Bhuniti-Lab is an enterprise-grade, nation-scale land governance technology platform engineered to transform Indian land administration, titling policy evaluation, and spatial land dispute resolution. Designed in strategic alignment with India's Viksit Bharat 2047 vision, Bhuniti-Lab bridges the long-standing structural divide between GIS spatial parcel mapping, econometric policy evaluation, judicial legal intelligence, and executive decision-making. Land in India accounts for over 66 percent of all pending civil litigation, locking up hundreds of billions of dollars in economic value and stalling critical national infrastructure projects across key state growth corridors. Traditional land governance systems suffer from fragmented spatial data, lack of standardized ISO 19152 Land Administration Domain Model (LADM) compliance, reliance on static historical revenue records without real-time satellite verification, and an utter inability to evaluate the true causal impact of land policy interventions across diverse districts.

Bhuniti-Lab solves these multi-dimensional governance challenges by integrating five core technology pillars into a single unified web platform:

First, a GIS Spatial Digital Twin built on high-resolution Leaflet satellite imagery, Local Government Directory (LGD) district boundary GeoJSON overlays, multispectral land cover indices (NDVI and NDBI), cadastral mismatch heatmaps, and SVAMITVA drone survey data layers. This provides surveyors, urban planners, revenue officers, and district collectors with precise spatial visibility into boundary discrepancies, climate risks, urban sprawl, and infrastructure corridor overlaps.

Second, a Causal Econometrics Engine (PolicyTwin) that brings rigorous statistical analysis and empirical econometrics to public policy. It implements four state-of-the-art econometric models: Staggered Difference-in-Differences (DiD) for evaluating multi-period policy rollouts; Synthetic Control Method (SCM) with Abadie-Diamond-Hainmueller convex donor pool weights for single-unit policy interventions; Sun and Abraham (2021) dynamic event-study estimators to capture treatment effect heterogeneity; and Calonico-Cattaneo-Titiunik spatial Regression Discontinuity Design (RDD) to detect policy jumps across administrative borders alongside McCrary density tests for manipulation checks.

Third, a Legal NLP and Dispute Intelligence Pipeline powered by fine-tuned DeBERTa-v3 natural language processing models. It automatically parses Indian high court and district court land judgments, extracts key legal entities (parties, survey numbers, statutory acts, and precedents), categorizes disputes into LADM-standard dispute types, and computes delay risk scores to predict judicial backlog bottlenecks.

Fourth, an Evidence Network and Claim Graph visualizer using D3.js force-directed graph layouts. It maps complex multi-party land claims, historical land grant dockets, and ownership chain nodes, allowing legal experts and judges to identify evidence gaps and evaluate claim strength scores from A+ to C.

Fifth, a Federated Policy Copilot powered by Google Gemini API integration and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) over official land dockets. It provides policy makers with context-aware, cited answers backed by SHA-256 document verification hashes, coupled with an interactive Ex-Ante What-If Policy Simulator that projects future litigation and revenue impact based on adjustable policy levers.

In addition, Bhuniti-Lab incorporates a multi-role RBAC access system (Cabinet Administrator, Judiciary Officer, Cadastral Surveyor, and Public Citizen), multi-lingual support across five Indian languages (English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Marathi), an OGC API Features (ISO 19168) and LADM GraphQL sandbox, and an automated jsPDF Cabinet Policy Brief generator. By unifying spatial analytics, legal NLP, causal inference, and generative AI, Bhuniti-Lab establishes a gold standard for transparent, evidence-based land governance in modern India.

The technical implementation encompasses 21 pull requests and 215 structured commits covering the full stack from TypeScript domain types, Three.js 3D wireframe visualization, D3 claim network rendering, Leaflet map layers, econometric matrix math, DeBERTa NLP classification, Gemini RAG integration, to localized i18n dictionaries. The platform delivers instant executive visibility, scientific policy validation, and automated PDF export capabilities for high-level Cabinet briefings. Through this holistic architecture, Bhuniti-Lab empowers state governments and national ministries to transition from reactive dispute management to proactive, data-driven, and dispute-free land administration.

In terms of measurable impact, Bhuniti-Lab reduces land title verification cycles from months to seconds, provides empirical causal proofs of land policy efficacy, streamlines judicial case scheduling, and unlocks agricultural and commercial credit for millions of property owners. As India moves towards its centenary of independence in 2047, Bhuniti-Lab provides the institutional, spatial, and analytical infrastructure necessary to underpin equitable economic prosperity, secure land rights, and rapid infrastructure expansion nationwide.

Furthermore, the architecture guarantees absolute compliance with international spatial data standards (ISO 19152 LADM, ISO 19168 OGC API Features) while ensuring local applicability across state-specific revenue acts (such as the Maharashtra Land Revenue Code, Karnataka Land Revenue Act, Tamil Nadu Land Reforms Act, and Uttar Pradesh Revenue Code). By establishing an open, scalable, and secure software environment, Bhuniti-Lab bridges the gap between ground survey realities, legal courtrooms, academic econometric research, and top-level executive governance.
